{#- Ginger's face. Shared by the control page and the login screen. Eyelids use the standard character-animation approach: each eye is a clipping mask, and the lid is a plain rectangle that slides up and down *inside* that mask. Because the lid can only ever paint within the eye shape, it can never spill onto the forehead as a scowl or creep over the ears — which is exactly what a free-floating lid shape did. Everything is rounded: the head is an organic wider-than-tall path, and the ears, inner ears, nose and bowtie are drawn with a same-colour stroke and round line joins so no corner comes to a point. -#}
